# Recording the demo

The page performs its own 60-second tour with on-screen captions, so a screen
recording needs no voiceover and comes out identical every time.

## Run it

Open the live URL with `#demo` on the end:

    https://YOUR-URL/#demo

It starts after ~1s. Or click the ▶ button in the sidebar footer.
It stops on its own after 60s and hands control back.
Dragging the scene at any point cancels the tour, so don't touch the mouse.

## Before you hit record

- **Window at 1400px wide or more.** Below 1040px the layout reflows to
  stage-on-top, which reads badly in a 16:9 crop.
- **Hide your bookmarks bar and go full screen.** ⌃⌘F in most browsers.
- The tour dims the insight cards and the legend on its own, so the frame
  stays clean without you doing anything.

## Capture

macOS: ⇧⌘5 → Record Selected Portion → drag around the browser content only,
not the chrome. Or OBS at 1920x1080, 60fps, for a sharper result.

## The 13 beats

| Time | What is on screen |
| ---- | ----------------- |
| 0:00 | Blend label, wide. "A proprietary blend hides the dose of everything inside it." |
| 0:04 | Slow orbit. "But the label must list them heaviest first." |
| 0:08 | Push in, L-Citrulline selected, derivation appears. "So the ceiling is arithmetic." |
| 0:12 | "L-Citrulline sits 2nd in a 2,400 mg blend." |
| 0:16 | "It cannot exceed 1,200 mg. A dose is 6,000." |
| 0:20 | Pull back to the mass check. "Now weigh the whole formula." |
| 0:24 | "Dosing it properly needs 13,616 mg. The scoop holds 9,500." |
| 0:29 | "It does not fit inside its own scoop." — red |
| 0:34 | Greens powder loads. "0 of 9 at an effective dose." |
| 0:39 | Disclosed label loads. "7 of 7." |
| 0:44 | "Every dose stated. Every one in range." |
| 0:48 | Blend returns, editor opens. "Put your own label in and check it." |
| 0:53 | "Free. Open source. Nothing leaves your browser." |

## Cuts to make from the one take

- **X / LinkedIn** — the whole 60s, 16:9, as-is.
- **Reels / TikTok / Shorts** — crop 9:16 centred on the vials. The sidebar
  falls outside the frame; the captions and the vial labels carry the story on
  their own. Trim to 0:00–0:34 if you want it under 40s — that ends on
  "It does not fit inside its own scoop," which is the strongest stopping point.
- **Still for the OG image** — pause at 0:12 or 0:36, crop to 1200x630.

## Voiceover script

Thirteen lines, each cued to a beat of the guided tour. Timings are seconds from the
start of the 60-second take. Every line has at least 0.6s of headroom before the next
cue, so a natural read will fit without rushing.

| Cue | Line |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00.4 | A proprietary blend hides the dose of everything inside it. |
| 0:04.6 | But the label still has to list them heaviest first. |
| 0:08.6 | So the ceiling is just arithmetic. |
| 0:12.4 | L citrulline is second in a twenty four hundred milligram blend. |
| 0:16.4 | It cannot exceed twelve hundred. Trials use six thousand. |
| 0:20.9 | Now weigh the whole formula. |
| 0:24.9 | Together, the studied ranges need thirteen thousand milligrams. |
| 0:29.9 | The scoop holds nine and a half. It does not fit. |
| 0:34.4 | A greens powder. None of its nine reach their range. |
| 0:39.4 | A fully disclosed label. Seven out of seven. |
| 0:44.4 | Every dose stated. Every one of them in range. |
| 0:48.9 | Put your own label in, and check it. |
| 0:53.9 | Free, open source, and nothing leaves your browser. |

Record it in one take against the silent 16:9 cut. If a line lands slightly late it
still works — the captions on screen carry the meaning, the voice is reinforcement.
